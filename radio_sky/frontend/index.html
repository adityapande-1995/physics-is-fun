What are you looking at?

The orange glow in the map is 408 MHz radio continuum — synchrotron radiation from relativistic electrons spiralling in galactic magnetic fields. The bright ridge along the galactic plane is the dusty, optically-invisible heart of the Milky Way. Click any source to learn why it glows in radio but is hidden from your eyes.
